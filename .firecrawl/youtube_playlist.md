Does your car's steering wheel feel crooked or often veer to the left or right when you let go? Don't ignore it! ⚠️

That's one of the main signs that your car needs wheel alignment. Besides unstable steering, another sign you can check directly is the physical condition of the tires, which are starting to wear or "eaten" on one side (only bald on the inside or outside).

Remember, the myth that wheel alignment is only done once when changing tires is COMPLETELY WRONG. Ideally, perform wheel alignment maintenance every 6 months or twice a year to maintain driving stability and extend the life of your tires.
