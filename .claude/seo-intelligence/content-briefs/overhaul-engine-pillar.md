### 8. Overhaul vs Semi Overhaul

**H2: Perbedaan Overhaul vs Semi Overhaul**

| Aspek | Overhaul Lengkap | Semi Overhaul |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|
| Scope | Full engine teardown | Upper engine only |
| Duration | 7-14 hari | 3-5 hari |
| Cost | Rp 8-25 juta+ | Rp 3-8 juta |
| Komponen | Semua internal | Head, valve, gasket |
| Kapan | Ring aus, bore macet | Valve seal, head gasket |
| Warranty | 6-12 bulan | 3-6 bulan |

**When to choose Overhaul:**
- Ring piston aus
- Cylinder bore rusak
- Crankshaft bearing aus
- Semua komponen aus

**When to choose Semi Overhaul:**
- Valve seal bocor saja
- Head gasket rusak
- Timing chain/belt perlu ganti
- Budget terbatas
