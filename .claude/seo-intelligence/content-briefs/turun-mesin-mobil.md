### 5. Proses Turun Mesin

**H2: Proses Turun Mesin Step-by-Step (KO-009)**

**Part 1: Persiapan**

1. **Persiapan Tools**
   - Engine hoist / cherry picker
   - Engine stand
   - Transmission jack
   - Basic socket set
   - Torque wrench
   - Supporting equipment

2. **Persiapan Vehicle**
   - Parkir di area rata
   - Lepas battery (- terminal first)
   - Drain coolant
   - Drain engine oil
   - Drain transmission fluid (if needed)

3. **Dokumentasi**
   - Foto semua koneksi
   - Catat posisi komponen
   - Label harness dan hose

**Part 2: Remove Components**

4. **Remove Upper Components**
   - Hood (if needed)
   - Air intake system
   - Intake manifold
   - Exhaust manifold
   - Radiator + hoses
   - AC compressor (if attached)
   - Power steering pump (if attached)

5. **Remove Electrical**
   - All sensors connectors
   - Starter motor
   - Alternator
   - Wiring harness connections
   - Ground straps

6. **Remove Fluids Lines**
   - Fuel lines
   - Coolant hoses
   - Oil passages
   - Transmission lines (if applicable)

**Part 3: Engine Removal**

7. **Support Engine**
   - Attach engine hoist
   - Take up slack tension
   - Verify secure connection

8. **Remove Engine Mounts**
   - Front mount
   - Rear mount
   - Side mount(s)
   - All torque rods/stays

9. **Clearances**
   - Check all connections cleared
   - Verify no pinned wires/hoses
   - Clear transmission crossmember
   - Ensure clearance from body

10. **Lift Engine**
    - Slowly take up weight
    - Guide engine out
    - Clear firewall
    - Clear hood hinge area
    - Lower to engine stand or floor

**Part 4: Post-Removal**

11. **Secure Engine**
    - Mount to engine stand OR
    - Place on clean surface with support

12. **Protect Components**
    - Cover open ports
    - Seal openings
    - Cover transmission if exposed

**Duration: 4-6 jam untuk full drop (expert technician)**

- Diagram: Engine removal process
- Tools checklist
- Time per step

### 7. Tips

**H2: Tips Penting Sebelum Turun Mesin**

1. **Second Opinion**
   > Dapatkan diagnosis kedua sebelum menyetujui turun mesin. Tidak semua bengkel memiliki keahlian yang sama.

2. **Clarify Service**
   > Pastikan tahu service apa yang akan dilakukan SETELAH turun. Turun mesin adalah biaya tambahan.

3. **Get Written Estimate**
   > Mintaestimasi tertulis yang mencakup: biaya turun, service, spare part, dan garansi.

4. **Documentation**
   > Foto/video kondisi mesin sebelum turun untuk referensi.

5. **Timeline**
   > Tanyakan estimasi waktu total: turun + service + pasang + testing.

6. **Warranty**
   > Pastikan ada garansi untuk pekerjaan yang dilakukan.

7. **Shop Experience**
   > Pilih bengkel dengan pengalaman spesifik untuk mobil Anda.

**Warning Signs (Rule R7):**
> ⚠️ "Jika bengkel tidak bisa menjelaskan dengan jelas为什么要 turun mesin, dapatkan opinion kedua."
