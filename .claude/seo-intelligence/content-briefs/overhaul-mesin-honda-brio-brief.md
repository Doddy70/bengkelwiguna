### 3. Common Problems & Symptoms (400 words)

**3.1 Oil Consumption (EAT OLI)**
```
Symptoms:
- Oli turun cepat (bisa 1L per 1000km)
- Asap biru saat gas
- Catalytic converter rusak
Causes:
- Ring piston worn (L-series known issue)
- Piston ring gap tidak sesuai
- Oil control ring failure
```

**3.2 Overheating Issues**
```
Symptoms:
- Temperature naik saat macet
- Kipas tidak nyala
- Coolant habis
Causes:
- Thermostat stuck
- Water pump lemah
- Radiator clog
```

**3.3 Hard Starting**
```
Symptoms:
- Sulit start pagi
- Perlu gas saat start
- rpm tidak stabil
Causes:
- Fuel pump lemah
- Fuel injector clog
- Spark plug worn
```

### 8. FAQ Section (150 words)

```faq
Q: Berapa lama overhaul mesin Brio?
```
